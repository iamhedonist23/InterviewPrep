Implement PHASE 8 ONLY.

Goal:
Prepare the website for Google AdSense without harming user experience.

IMPORTANT:
This phase does NOT guarantee Google AdSense approval.
Build the website to follow Google's policies and best practices.

Create reusable ad components:

* AdBanner
* AdInArticle
* AdSidebar
* AdResponsive

Environment variables:

NEXT_PUBLIC_ADSENSE_CLIENT_ID
NEXT_PUBLIC_ADSENSE_ENABLED

Development:
Ads disabled by default.

Production:
Ads enabled only when configured.

Do not hardcode a real publisher ID.

Ad rules:

1. Never encourage users to click ads.
2. Never place ads where they can be mistaken for navigation.
3. Never place ads directly over controls.
4. Never interfere with practice questions.
5. Avoid excessive ad density.
6. Keep ads clearly distinguishable.
7. Do not show ads on private/admin pages.
8. Do not create pages purely to show ads.

Suggested placements:

Homepage:

* Between major sections

Question page:

* After useful content sections
* Near the end

Article:

* After introduction
* Mid-content where appropriate
* Near end

Desktop:

* Limited sidebar placement

Mobile:

* Conservative responsive placements

Create AdSense script loading safely.

Do not break rendering if AdSense is unavailable.

Add configuration comments.

Also verify:

* Privacy policy
* Cookie policy
* Terms
* Disclaimer
* Contact
* About

Make sure these pages are linked in the footer.

Run production build.

Do not claim AdSense approval.
