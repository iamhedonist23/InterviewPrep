JAVA INTERVIEW QUESTIONS — VERIFIED + HIGH-CONFIDENCE SET

PURPOSE This is a high-confidence interview bank. It separates questions
explicitly reported in published interview-experience reports from
recurring/common interview questions.

LABELS [REPORTED] = a published interview experience explicitly
describes the question or task. Wording is lightly normalized and is not
presented as a verbatim quotation. [COMMON] = a recurring interview
question supported by current interview-question references and multiple
interview patterns; it is not claimed to be tied to one specific
interview.

IMPORTANT Do not market this file as ‘questions guaranteed to be asked’.
Interview questions vary by company, role, seniority, resume and
interviewer. Recommended website wording: ‘Curated interview questions
based on reported interview experiences and commonly tested interview
patterns.’
