C++ INTERVIEW QUESTIONS — STRICTLY EVIDENCE-BASED

Purpose

This bank is intentionally strict. It contains questions that are
explicitly reported in published candidate interview experiences. The
wording has been lightly normalized for clarity; the underlying
question/topic is taken from the cited interview report. This is NOT a
synthetic “commonly asked” question bank.

Evidence labels

[REPORTED] = Explicitly described in a published candidate interview
report. [REPEATED] = The same or substantially the same topic appears in
multiple independent interview reports. [CODING] = A coding/task
question explicitly reported in an interview.

IMPORTANT

Do not market every question as a verbatim transcript. The wording below
is normalized from candidate reports. Company/source attribution is
included so users can verify the evidence.
