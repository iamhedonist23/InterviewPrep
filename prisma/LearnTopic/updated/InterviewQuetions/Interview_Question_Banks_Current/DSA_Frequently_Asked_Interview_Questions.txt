DSA INTERVIEW QUESTIONS — FREQUENTLY ASKED / HIGH-CONFIDENCE

Purpose

This is a practical DSA interview-preparation bank covering questions
and problem patterns that recur frequently across software engineering
interviews.

IMPORTANT: These are NOT claimed to be verbatim questions from one
particular candidate’s interview. They are normalized interview-style
questions based on recurring DSA interview patterns and established
interview-question references. Do not market this file as “every
question was literally asked.”

Recommended website wording: “Curated DSA interview questions based on
frequently tested interview patterns and established interview-question
resources.”
