DSA REAL INTERVIEW QUESTIONS — EVIDENCE-BASED BANK

Definition

This file contains DSA/coding questions that are explicitly described in
published candidate interview experiences.

This is deliberately NOT a generic AI-generated question list.

Evidence labels

[REPORTED] = The candidate explicitly reported being asked the
problem/topic. [REPEATED] = The same problem/topic is reported in
multiple interview experiences. [VARIANT] = A reported interview problem
that is a variation of a known problem.

Important: The wording below may be normalized for readability. It
should not be described as a verbatim transcript unless the cited source
uses exactly that wording.
