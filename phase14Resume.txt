==================================================
OWNERSHIP AND SECURITY
==================================================

A user must only be able to access their own resumes.

Server-side authorization is mandatory.

Never trust a userId supplied by the browser.

Always determine the authenticated user from the session.

Prevent:

- Reading another user's resume
- Updating another user's resume
- Deleting another user's resume
- Exporting another user's resume
